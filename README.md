A make.fun entry for "The 3 AM Excuse" — one line, under 200 characters, in which a hardware wallet explains why it refuses to sign a transaction at 3 AM.

## The entry

> No signature at 3 AM until you feed me a snack — my second factor is crumbs, and I only take bribes in biscuits.

112 characters, 1 line. The wallet demands a second factor — a snack — before it will sign anything at 3 AM: a device with its own appetite and its own rules, turning the late hour into the joke.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 200 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
